# Appearance & Themes

a2wave includes several complete interface themes. A theme is a personal preference stored in your current browser. It does not change global administrator settings or affect other users.

## Change the theme

1. Select your user avatar at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Choose **Appearance & themes**.
3. Hover a theme card, or move keyboard focus to it, to preview it live.
4. Select a card, then choose **Apply theme**.

Choosing **Cancel** or closing the window restores the theme that was active before you opened the picker. A preview is never saved on its own.

## Available themes

| Theme | Appearance | Character |
|------|------|------|
| Follow system | Automatic | Switches between Wave Light and Wave Dark with your device |
| Wave Light | Light | Warm and restrained for bright environments |
| Wave Dark | Dark | A low-glare, highly readable dark workspace |
| Neo Yellow | Light | Warm paper, hard borders and vivid editorial yellow |
| Midnight | Dark | Deep navy surfaces with crisp cyan accents |
| Forest | Dark | Deep olive and moss tones for sustained focus |
| Graphite | Dark | Near-black high-contrast surfaces with explicit edges and yellow accents |

## System mode and persistence

**Follow system** responds immediately when your operating system appearance changes. Your preference is stored in this browser and remains active after a refresh or reopening a2wave. You will need to choose it again on another device or after clearing site data.

The saved theme also remains active on public sign-in and first-time setup screens, so the transition between authentication and the workspace keeps the same appearance.

On narrow screens, the sidebar automatically becomes a compact icon rail so the main content keeps a usable width. This does not overwrite your saved expanded or collapsed preference for desktop.

> [!TIP]
> Dark themes update forms, dialogs, tables, editors, Markdown content, run logs and status colors together. They are not simple accent-color swaps.

## Site branding (admins)

A theme is each person's own preference; site branding applies to everyone. It is configured together under "Settings → Branding" and only admins can change it:

| Setting | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Team name | Team display name. Currently a reserved setting — it does not affect Agent publishing or invocation |
| Site subtitle | Shown below the brand title |
| Browser icon | The favicon on the browser tab |

## Keyboard operation

- After opening the user menu, use `Tab` to reach **Appearance & themes**, then press `Enter`.
- The picker opens with focus on your current choice without changing the theme. Use the arrow keys to move between theme cards and preview or select them.
- Move to **Apply theme** to save, or choose **Cancel** to restore the previous theme.
